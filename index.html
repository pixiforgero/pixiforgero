How it works

One studio, every step

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us what you need, sizes, colors, quantity and deadline.

  2. 02

    Image processing

    We clean up and prepare your photos, sketches or logos for modelling.

  3. 03

    Modelling & rendering

    You approve a rendered preview before anything is printed.

  4. 04

    Multicolor printing

    Printed in several colors, with settings tuned to the use case.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Support removal, sanding, painting or assembly, then careful packing.
