Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

What do I need to send?

A sentence is enough to start. A sketch, photo, logo or an existing 3D file (STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP) makes the quote faster and more accurate.

How does pricing work?

Every project is quoted individually, based on size, colors, material, finishing and quantity. We reply by email with the price and next steps before you commit to anything.

Will I see the result before it is printed?

Yes. For modelled pieces you approve a rendered preview first, and we adjust it until you are happy.

What is the largest order you take?

One-offs up to short series of 100 pieces. Need more? Ask anyway and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.

Which materials do you use?

Common choices are PLA and PETG, among others. Tell us what the part is for and we recommend the right one.

How long does it take?

It depends on size, quantity and finishing, so we confirm a delivery date in your quote.
